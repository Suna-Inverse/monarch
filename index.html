How it works

Install once, automate everything

A small menu-bar app runs the bridge and installs a plugin into each Adobe app for you.

Sign in

Open Monarch and sign in with the email address your invite went to. A 6-digit code arrives by email; no password.

Install the plugins

Press Install next to each Adobe app in the Monarch menu-bar app.

Connect to Monarch

Open the panel in any app. The badge switches to Serving the Monarch once it's connected.

Run the batch

Point a tool at a folder, a CSV or a selection, and let it work through hundreds of files.

Get Monarch

For invited members. The app is free to download; the plugins unlock when you sign in with the email address your invite went to. No invite yet? Request access.

Download for Mac

macOS · Apple Silicon and Intel

  1. Open Monarch.dmg and drag Monarch into Applications.
  2. Open Monarch. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in with your email address and the 6-digit code you receive.
