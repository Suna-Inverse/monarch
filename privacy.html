Privacy

Your data and Monarch

Monarch keeps only what it needs to give you access and to switch it off again. No tracking, no ads, and nothing is sold or shared.

Last updated 8 October 2026

Who is responsible

Monarch is a personal project, made and run by its developer. For anything about your data, email hello@sunainverse.com.

What Monarch stores

WhatDetailsKept
Your accountYour name, email address, whether your access is switched on, and whether you are an admin.Until you are removed
Your MacsThe name the Mac reports, when it was signed in and when it last checked in. Used to keep the limit of 2 Macs per person.Until you are removed
Browser sign-insFor the Tools guide: a short description of your browser and when you signed in.30 days
Sign-in codesThe 6-digit code from your email, stored scrambled so nobody can read it.10 minutes, deleted once used
Access requestsWhen you ask for access: your name, email address, team or company and your message.Until approved or declined, at most 90 days
Code requestsYour email address and the time you asked for a code, to stop abuse.1 hour

Sign-in keys for your Macs and browser are also stored only in scrambled form. Even with a copy of the database, nobody could sign in as you.

What Monarch never stores

  • Your documents, images, files or anything you make with the plugins. They stay on your Mac.
  • Your IP address or location. The Monarch server doesn't save them.
  • Analytics, tracking pixels or advertising data. There aren't any.

Cookies

The site uses two cookies, both only to keep you signed in. They can't be read by scripts on the page and aren't used for anything else, so there's no cookie banner.

  • monarch_web: keeps you signed in to the Tools guide, for 30 days.
  • monarch_admin: only for the admin, for 7 days.

On your Mac

The Monarch app keeps its sign-in key, a 24-hour access pass and a small log file on your Mac, so Monarch keeps working when the connection drops for a while. Signing out in the app ends the sign-in on the server, so that key stops working.

Services that help run Monarch

These services handle data on Monarch's behalf. Like any web service, they may log technical details such as IP addresses under their own privacy policies.

  • Cloudflare runs the Monarch server and database (stored in the EU) and forwards email sent to hello@sunainverse.com. Privacy policy
  • Resend sends the sign-in and invite emails, so it handles your email address and those messages. Privacy policy
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app download. Privacy policy
  • Google Fonts provides the font this site uses, so your browser loads it from Google. Privacy policy

Why

Your data is used only to give Monarch to the people who have access to it: to handle your access request, to sign you in, to keep the 2-Mac limit, to send your codes and invites, and to switch access off when needed. Monarch is not a commercial product and your data is never used for anything else.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, or have your account and Macs removed completely. Email hello@sunainverse.com and you'll get an answer within a month. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can also contact the Dutch privacy authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

When Monarch starts storing something new, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes.
