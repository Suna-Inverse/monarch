About

Hi, I'm Suna.

Designer and tinkerer from the Netherlands. Dominating the busywork since forever.

For almost 20 years I've worked in print and digital production: campaign publications, in-store print and screens, often in dozens of versions at once.

I've always tinkered on the side. Every repetitive job got its own little shortcut, until the shortcuts started talking to each other. Monarch is what happened next: a plugin for each Adobe app, and a bridge that lets them hand work to each other.

The names come from a lifelong love of games: every good game needs a ruler, and here the Monarch rules the batch work.

My favourite

Subject Claude

Monarch's Claude connection lets Claude direct the Adobe apps through the bridge. Ask for a change and watch InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop or After Effects carry it out. It's my favourite way to work with Monarch.
